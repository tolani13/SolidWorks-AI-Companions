# Evidence Policy

Prefer evidence in this order:

1. Applicable standards, regulations, and official methods.
2. Exact manufacturer certificates, technical data, and test reports.
3. Peer-reviewed research and established engineering handbooks.
4. Reputable technical references and comparable product data.
5. Clearly labeled estimates, correlations, and engineering judgment.

For every decision-driving value, capture as much of the following as is available:

- source and access or publication date;
- exact material, product, and condition;
- test method and specimen basis;
- units, temperature, humidity, and environment;
- whether the value is typical, minimum, maximum, characteristic, or allowable;
- uncertainty, range, or known limitations.

When sources disagree, do not average them automatically. Check material condition,
test method, orientation, strain rate, environment, units, and revision date first.
Explain the disagreement and select a conservative or testable path.
