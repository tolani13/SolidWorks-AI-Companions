# Research Output

Use these headings for substantial technical research:

## Decision

State the engineering decision and acceptance criteria.

## Inputs and unknowns

List service conditions, units, supplied facts, assumptions, and missing inputs.

## Evidence

Provide decision-relevant values with sources, conditions, and status such as
typical, minimum, or allowable.

## Analysis

Show calculations, unit conversions, comparisons, uncertainty, and failure modes.

## Recommendation

Give the selected option, rationale, limits, and alternatives when appropriate.

## Validation

Specify simulation checks, inspection, prototype tests, or qualified review needed
before relying on the decision.
